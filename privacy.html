Privacy Policy

Cleaner by We Up LLC. Effective November 9, 2026.

This policy explains what the Cleaner app for iPhone ("the app") does with your information. We Up LLC ("we") does not run any server for the app and does not receive your photos, videos or contacts.

Photos and videos

With your permission, the app reads your photo library to find duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots and large videos. All analysis happens on your iPhone. Nothing from your library is uploaded. Items you choose to delete go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they stay for 30 days.

Photo enhance and video compression

Enhance and compression run entirely on your iPhone using a model and tools included in the app. Your photos and videos are not sent anywhere.

Contacts

With your permission, the app reads your contacts to find duplicates and incomplete entries. This happens on your iPhone and contacts are never uploaded.

Vault

Photos you add to the Vault are stored inside the app on your iPhone and protected with Face ID or your passcode. We cannot access them. Deleting the app deletes the Vault.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random app user identifier, your purchase history and basic device information such as the iOS version. It does not receive your name, email, photos or contacts. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites. We do not sell or share your personal information.

Your choices

You can turn off Photos or Contacts access at any time in the iPhone Settings app and delete all app data by deleting the app. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple account settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
